# Art direction and imagery

## Before sourcing
Write a shot list with: subject, narrative function, crop/aspect ratio, lighting, palette, focal point, placement, mobile crop and source/license.
Aim for useful variety: wide environmental, medium activity, close detail, human presence, product/material where relevant. A page does not need more imagery simply to fill space.

## Selection
- Use actual project imagery when the user provides permission and assets.
- For fictional brands, use original generated or correctly licensed imagery with a clear disclosure when necessary.
- Track provenance/rights in the brand brief. Never claim a photo represents a real customer or real facility when invented.
- Avoid repetitive stock-looking portraits, inconsistent lenses, mixed warm/cool grades and visibly AI-distorted anatomy/equipment.
- For editorial sites, plan images as a narrative sequence, not independent section decorations.
- Test exact crop at 1440/768/390. Protect faces, instruments, signage and product details.
- Use appropriate image formats and sizes; check image decode and performance.

## Audit
For every key image ask: what new information or feeling does this convey? Does another image already do the same job? Is it truthful, consistent with the brand, licensed, legible behind any type, and resilient on mobile?
