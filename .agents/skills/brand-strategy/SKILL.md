# Brand strategy
1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `standards/workflow.md`, `brands/creative-matrix.md`.
2. Clarify the category, audience, commercial action and whether the brand is fictional.
3. Find a distinct creative thesis, not just a palette. Compare against existing brands and list at least three concrete differences.
4. Fill `brands/<slug>/brief.md` from `brands/BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md`.
5. Write authentic site copy outlines. Mark invented facts and avoid fake reviews, statistics, awards and client claims.
6. Hand the signed-off direction to editorial-art-direction and website-design. Do not generate a video.
Output: brand brief, differentiation, content map, open assumptions.
